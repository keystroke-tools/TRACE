THE QUICK ANSWER

Know what to try next.

TRACE turns raw simulator telemetry into synchronized, approachable evidence. It does not pretend a heuristic is a coach—and it never hides the underlying data.

01 / RECORD

Capture the whole drive

Record live driving or telemetry while Assetto Corsa plays a replay. Available laps, sectors, inputs, tyres, fuel, and native source data stay together locally.

Replay files must be played through AC
02 / COMPARE

Compare on one axis

Speed, throttle, brake, gear, steering, line, and cumulative delta remain synchronized as you seek or zoom through the lap.

Reference vs. analysed lap
03 / ANALYSE

Focus on the corners

Deterministic heuristics highlight measured differences in braking, minimum speed, and throttle so you can inspect the underlying telemetry yourself.

May be incorrect · not coaching advice
04 / SHARE
.trace

Bring another driver's session

Compact TRACE packages preserve attribution, laps, telemetry, and an explicitly confirmed setup for inspection and comparison on another machine.

One file · no separate database or Arrow file
A SESSION, NOT A SPREADSHEET

Everything from the run,
kept in context.

Review a long race without turning the session page into a wall of rows. Open any lap for its recorded telemetry and the fuel, tyre, validity, or sector evidence the simulator made available.

  • ✓ Searchable sessions, names, tags, and driver attribution
  • ✓ Fastest laps, saved comparisons, and theoretical best
  • ✓ Compatible setup matching and readable AC setup differences
  • ✓ Missing or uncertain simulator data stays explicit
FROM TRACK TO ANSWER

Three steps. No telemetry degree required.

01

Record

Open TRACE, start Assetto Corsa, then drive or play a replay at normal speed. TRACE stores completed sessions locally.

02

Choose a reference

Use your fastest lap, a lap from an imported friend's session, or one of TRACE's compatible suggestions.

03

Follow the difference

Scrub once across every graph and the track map. Zoom from the full lap down to a sector or corner.

LLM

Developed with LLM assistance

Large language models are used as development tools for research, planning, code generation, review, testing, and documentation under human direction. TRACE does not use an LLM to generate its telemetry analysis or driving conclusions; those remain deterministic and inspectable.
